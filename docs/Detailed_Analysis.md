# Detailed Analysis – Screen-by-Screen Breakdown
## 1. KPI Summary Panel

This panel shows the high-level health of the business in one place. I used simple text cards because they’re easy to read and don’t confuse the user with too many charts. These KPIs helped me quickly understand the overall trend before exploring deeper views.
## 2. Total MRR Trend Chart

Here I used a simple bar chart to show how our monthly recurring revenue is growing month by month. 
I kept the chart simple — one measure only — to highlight the upward trend clearly.
The color gradient also helps in showing the improvement over time. This chart basically tells how fast 
the business is moving forward in terms of revenue.

## 3. Monthly Revenue & Retention Trend

This section mixes a bar chart for revenue and a line chart for Net RCR (%) on the same view. 
I used dual-axis because it helps compare money movement with retention quality in one place. 
The bars show how revenue is increasing every month, while the red line shows how retention 
is shifting. This view helped me check whether revenue growth is driven by healthy customer retention or by expansion in a few accounts.

## 4. MRR Movement Breakdown

This section helped me understand what is actually driving the revenue every month. I broke MRR into three parts:

New MRR – Revenue coming from brand new customers

Expansion MRR – Revenue increasing because existing customers upgraded or bought more

Churn MRR – Revenue lost when customers cancel or downgrade

I used a stacked line/area chart so I can see all three movements in one place.
This view made it easy to check if the company is growing because of new customers or because old customers are expanding.
It also helped me notice if churn is rising in any month.

## 5. Cohort Retention – MRR Heatmap

This part of the dashboard helped me understand how well we are keeping customers month over month. I used cohort analysis because it shows how customers behave after signing up, not only how much revenue we make.

The heatmap layout makes it easy to follow retention:

Rows → signup month (cohorts)

Columns → months since signup

Color → how much MRR stays from the original baseline

Dark colors mean higher retention, light colors mean lower retention.

This view helped me see:

if new customers are staying longer

if older cohorts are improving or dropping

if MRR retention is strong or weak

how long it takes before customers churn completely

This is one of the most important charts because it tells if the business model is healthy or not.
