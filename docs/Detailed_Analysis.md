# Detailed Analysis – Screen-by-Screen Breakdown
## 1. KPI Summary Panel

This part of dashboard shows high-level health of business in one place. I used simple text cards because they are easy to read and they don’t confuse user with too much graph. These KPIs helped me understand overall trend quickly before going inside deep charts.
## 2. Total MRR Trend Chart

Here I used a simple bar chart to show how our monthly recurring revenue is growing month by month. 
I kept the design clean with only one measure because I wanted to highlight the upward trend clearly. 
The color gradient also helps in showing the improvement over time. This chart basically tells how fast 
the business is moving forward in terms of revenue.
## 3. Monthly Revenue & Retention Trend

This section mixes a bar chart for revenue and a line chart for Net RCR (%) on the same view. 
I used dual-axis because it helps compare money movement with retention quality in one place. 
The bars show how revenue is increasing every month, while the red line shows how retention 
is shifting. This view helped me understand if revenue is growing because of healthy customers 
or because of expansion coming from few accounts.
